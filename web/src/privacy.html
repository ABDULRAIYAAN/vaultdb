Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

This site is a demo of VaultDB, a personal open-source project by Abdul Raiyaan. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. This page explains the little it does handle.

What the playground stores

  • Your sandbox. The commands you type and the data they create are kept in the server's memory, in a database only your browser can reach. Nothing is written to disk. The sandbox is deleted after 30 minutes without use, or when you press "Empty my sandbox".
  • One cookie. A random identifier named vaultdb_sandbox connects your browser to your sandbox. It is not used for anything else, is not readable by scripts, and expires after 30 minutes.
  • Your IP address is held in memory for a few minutes to limit how fast commands can be sent, which protects the server from abuse. It is not logged or stored.

Ask the docs

Questions you type into "Ask the docs" are used to search VaultDB's own documentation. If this server is set up with an AI model to write answers, your question and the passages found are sent to that model's provider to produce the answer, under that provider's own privacy terms. Do not type personal information into questions.

What is not collected

No names, emails, analytics, fingerprinting or third-party scripts. Fonts and all other files are served from this site, so your browser does not contact other companies when you visit.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
